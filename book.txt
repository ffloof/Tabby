w e2e4 e7e5 d2d4 e5d4 c2c3 d4c3 f1c4

Against the sicilian we play the smith morra

w e2e4 c7c5 d2d4 c5d4 c2c3


As Black

Against e4 we play the sicilian
b e2e4 c7c5

Against d4 we play benoni/benko gambits/and similar lines with 1.c5
